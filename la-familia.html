✦ Community ✦

La Familia.

A living circle between the walks. A place to stay in touch, to remember, and to remind each other. A place where what was felt can stay alive, find its way into daily life, and continue to take shape together.

✦ What La Familia is

A place to stay close
to what opened.

For many people, what they experience in these walks is something they may only encounter once in their lives. La Familia is a place to stay connected with the people they shared that experience with — to keep that sense of closeness alive and to continue sharing. Sometimes, new walks emerge from that connection. It is also a place for those who haven’t joined yet but feel curious. A way to hear the stories directly from the people who lived them, to imagine what it might feel like, and to sense whether something in it speaks to them.

✦ What comes each month
✦ A lived story

Each month, we begin with a lived story. A moment from a walk, told by someone who was there.

✦ A place or a harvest

That story takes us to a place — sometimes a country, sometimes a harvest — and it shapes how the month unfolds.

✦ Notes from the land

From there, we share what belongs to that place: stories, ways of seeing, music, teachings, sometimes even recipes.

✦ Practices & reflection

There are always practices — for the body or the mind — and moments to reflect. Occasionally, we gather live.

✦ How we stay with it
For those who walked
A way to keep the thread alive with the people you shared the experience with.
For those who are curious
A way to hear directly from the people who lived it, and sense whether something in it moves you.
For daily life
Small practices and reflections that help what opened find a place in the body, the mind, and the day.
For future walks
Sometimes the connection continues into a new walk, a harvest, or a gathering.
For now
This space is open. Over time, it may become a smaller circle for those who want to stay closer to this work.
