Merve

I dance, I travel, and I make space for people to come home to themselves.

My path has led me across continents and thresholds — from the red mountains of Peru to the banks of the Ganges, from silent monasteries to the deserts of Egypt. Now rooted in Portugal, I create spaces where the body becomes a language of transformation.

I'm contemplative, but I'm also playful. I laugh a lot. Depth and lightness belong in the same room.
