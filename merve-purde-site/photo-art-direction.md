# Photography — Art Direction & Shot List

*For Merve's shoot. The single biggest lever in the whole project. Every image on the site is a slot waiting for one of these.*

**Design context:** the site is now white / gallery / art-book with a single electric-cobalt accent and an elegant serif. So the photography carries ALL the color and warmth — it should feel like images hung in a bright contemporary gallery, not a warm retreat brochure.

## The one rule
**Shoot the light, not the pose.** Everything should look *caught*, not staged — morning/late-afternoon sun, real movement, real laughter. If a frame looks like a "wellness brand," delete it. If it looks like a page from a contemporary art or travel magazine, keep it.

## The feeling in five words
Sunlit · alive · spacious · human · warm.

## Light
Golden, directional, natural — early morning or the last two hours before sunset; big soft window light indoors. High-key and airy; warm open shadows, never crushed black. Chase real sun: a shaft across a studio floor, light through linen, dappled shade. **Avoid:** flat grey, hard midday, tungsten "cosy" light, moody darkness, candle-mysticism.

## Palette to shoot into
Because the UI is near-white + cobalt, images can be vivid and full of life: warm skin, white plaster, olive/sage, sky-blue, terracotta *within the photo* (fine in a photograph — just not as the site's paint), natural linen, bare wood. Clean, bright, Mediterranean-morning — not forest-at-dusk.

## Wardrobe & bodies
Natural fabrics — linen, cotton, wool — off-white, clay, ochre, olive, soft rust. Movement-friendly. Real, relaxed, unposed bodies of different kinds. You *doing*, not *presenting*. Bare feet welcome.

## Framing & composition
Leave negative space (we crop into calm frames with room for big serif type — shoot loose). Mix scales: details (hands, feet, cloth, a cup) → mid (a body mid-movement) → wide (a small figure in a big sunlit room). Both orientations: verticals for portrait slots (4:5), wide horizontals for the full-bleed band (21:9). Motion is the signature — **let it blur a little**: a trailing hand, hair in air, cloth in motion.

## The shot list (mapped to the homepage slots)

**1 · Full-bleed band — "people in motion, light, laughter"** (wide 21:9)
A group moment: bodies moving together, genuine laughter, sun flare welcome. Wide and airy with space for text on one side. The "I want to stay" image.

**2 · Movement — "studio floor, bare feet, moving bodies"** (4:5)
Ground-level or mid: bare feet on warm wood/plaster, bodies in a workshop. Documentary, in-the-moment.

**3 · Relating — "two people, eye contact, soft light"** (4:5)
Two people in connection — foreheads close, hands, a held gaze. Tender, not staged-intimate. Consent and warmth.

**4 · Wisdom Walk / journey — "a trail, mountains, walking"** (4:5)
From your retreats/pilgrimages: a figure walking a landscape, small against big nature. Existing archive images welcome here.

**5 · Ritual — "hands, earth, quiet ritual"** (4:5)
Quiet and unposed: hands in soil, water, a shared meal. Everyday-sacred — **no** obvious "spiritual" props (no crystals/incense/mandalas on display).

**6 · Merve portrait — "you, laughing, real, in your own light"** (4:5)
The most important frame: *you*, warm and playful, mid-laugh or mid-thought, in beautiful natural light. Carries the depth-and-lightness of the whole brand. A moment, not a headshot.
