Mexico  ·  Mexico City · Oaxaca · Yucatán

Mexico Journey

12–20 November 2026  ·  9 days

A nine-day journey through three regions of Mexico, exploring the country’s living traditions, diverse landscapes, and rich cultural heritage.

From Mexico City and Teotihuacan, we travel to the Pacific coast of Mazunte and Oaxaca, before continuing to the Yucatán Peninsula — Chichén Itzá, cenotes, and Isla Mujeres.

Our days include Temazcal, movement and meditation practices, authentic relating, plant medicine and cacao ceremonies, archaeological sites, local markets, shared meals, and time to rest and integrate.

Guided by Merve Purde, together with Abuela Mari Carmen, Aldo Fogazza, and Tunay Kökden.
