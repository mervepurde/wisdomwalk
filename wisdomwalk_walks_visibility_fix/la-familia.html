✦   Community   ✦

La Familia.

A place to stay connected between walks. To ask questions, share what's moving, and walk with intention — even from home.

✦ What La Familia is

Not a newsletter.
A community.

For those who feel called to this kind of travel — whether you've walked with us before, or you're still finding your way toward it.

✦   What you receive
✦ Weekly field notes

A short letter from the path — a reflection, a story, a moment of quiet from the road.

✦ Live Q&A sessions

Open online sessions — ask anything about the walks, the lands, the practice. Real answers, real conversation.

✦ Early bird access

New walks open to circle members first — before public announcement, before the seats fill. Spaces are limited to 10–15 per journey.

✦ Members-only space

A quiet corner for members — past reflections, resources, and a way to stay connected between walks.
